Privacy

Time-Traveller is a free time-zone tool. You do not need to create an account or sign in to use it.

Information stored on your device

The home and destination locations you select may be saved in your browser's local storage so they are available when you return. You can clear this information through your browser's site-data settings.

Website hosting and analytics

Time-Traveller is hosted using Cloudflare and uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand website traffic. Cloudflare may process technical information needed to deliver and measure visits to the site. For details, see Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Google advertising and consent

Time-Traveller uses Google AdSense-related services. If advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may process information for ad delivery and measurement, subject to applicable consent choices. For details, see Google's advertising information.

Your choices

You can manage stored site data and relevant permissions through your browser. Where a consent message is presented, you can make your choices there.
