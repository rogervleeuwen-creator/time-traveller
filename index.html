A simple time-zone tool for travellers

Time-Traveller helps you compare the time at home with the time at your destination. Choose both locations and your travel date, then use the 24-hour timeline to see how the two local times line up throughout the day.

Why the travel date matters

Time differences are not always the same all year. Countries can change their clocks for daylight saving time on different dates, while some places do not change their clocks at all. Time-Traveller uses your selected date so the comparison reflects the time zones for that part of the year.

Reading the 24-hour timeline

The two timeline rows show the same moments in your home location and destination. This makes it easy to answer practical questions such as what time it will be at home when you have breakfast, arrive at your hotel, or want to call someone.

Best time to call home

The call-home section gives you a quick reference for 18:00 at your destination and shows the corresponding time at home. The full timeline remains available when another calling time suits you better.

Built to stay simple

Time-Traveller is a free browser-based travel tool. It does not require an account or sign-in. Its purpose is deliberately small: make the time difference between home and your destination easy to understand before and during a trip.

Privacy · About

Privacy

Time-Traveller does not require an account. Your selected locations can be stored locally in your browser so they are available when you return. The site uses Cloudflare for hosting and traffic measurement and Google services for advertising and consent management. Those services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies and the choices you make in the consent message.
